# Healthcare Insights Report
**Healthcare Patient Analytics** · Salma Akter · data Jan 2024 – Dec 2025

> All data is **synthetic and de-identified** (generated by `src/generate_data.py`); hospital names are
> fictional and no real patient information is used. Every number below is produced by `src/metrics.py`
> and matches the dashboard, the SQL queries and the Excel workbook.

## 1. Executive summary

| KPI | Value |

| Admissions (inpatient stays) | 21,723 (10,129 patients) |
| Average length of stay | 5.2 days (median 4.6) |
| Average treatment cost per stay | $33,094 (total $718.9M) |

**Three things to act on**

1. **Riverside Community Hospital** readmits 20.8% of patients (+7.3 pts
   vs Westbrook Specialty Hospital) and keeps them 1.1 days longer. Matching the median stay
   would free about **4,771 bed-days** over the two years.
2. **Patients aged 60+** use 29.7% of all services, stay 6.4 days on average and are
   readmitted 21.8% of the time: the group where discharge planning pays off most.
3. **No-shows rise with waiting time:** 5.3% for same-day bookings vs 21.0% when booked more
   than 30 days ahead, and 18.5% without an SMS reminder vs 12.6% with one.

## 6. Readmissions

* **16.2%** of stays were followed by another admission within 30 days (3,331 stays).
  Stays discharged in the final 30 days of data are excluded because their window is not complete.
* By age: 0-17 8.1% · 18-60 about 13.0% · **60+ 21.8%**.
* The discharge risk score separates patients well enough to act on: stays scored 25%+ (9.4% of stays)
  are readmitted **26.0%** of the time vs 15.2% for the rest. It is a screening
  tool (AUC ≈ 0.61), not a diagnosis.
* Bringing the rate down to the 14% target would mean about **229 fewer readmissions a year**.

* 18-30 year olds miss 20.8% of appointments; Mondays and Fridays are the worst days.

## 9. Recommendations

| Priority | Action | Evidence | Expected effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | Readmission review at Riverside Community Hospital (discharge checklist, follow-up clinic within 7 days) | 20.8% vs 13.5% at Westbrook Specialty Hospital; +1.1 days per stay | Closing half the gap ≈ 85 fewer readmissions/yr |
| High | Nurse call within 72 h for every stay with risk score ≥ 25% | 26.0% of these patients come back vs 15.2% | Targets the group most likely to return |
| High | SMS reminder for every appointment (today 28% get none) + a second reminder when booked 30+ days ahead | 18.5% vs 12.6% no-show; 21.0% for long waits | Recovers part of the 65 missed slots per week |
| Medium | Winter staffing plan for General Medicine and Pediatrics | +25% admissions Dec–Feb | Fewer delayed admissions in peak season |
| Medium | Geriatric discharge planning (medication review, home-care referral) | 60+: 21.8% readmitted, 6.4-day stays | Shorter stays and fewer returns |
| Low | Targeted overbooking for 18-30 on Monday/Friday slots | 20.8% no-show for 18-30 | Better clinic utilisation |

## 10. Method & data

* **Data:** synthetic hospital dataset (5 hospitals, 6 departments, Jan 2024 – Dec 2025) built on the columns of
  the original sample (age group, gender, region, department, treatment type, visit type, length of stay,
  treatment cost, recovery score, readmission risk) plus hospitals, appointments and actual readmissions.
* **Cleaning (Python):** 22 logged steps: duplicates, label variants, mixed date
  formats, text costs, swapped dates, percentage-typed risk, impossible age codes, missing values (see `docs/cleaning_log.csv`).
* **Database (PostgreSQL):** star schema, 18 business queries; the readmission rate is recomputed in SQL with
  `LEAD()` and matches Python exactly.
* **Excel:** the cleaning workbook recalculates every KPI with formulas (zero errors).
* **Limitations:** synthetic data shows realistic patterns but not real clinical outcomes; the readmission
  definition is all-cause (any return within 30 days, planned or not).
